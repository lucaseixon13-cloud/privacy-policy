# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TheNightShift collects, uses and stores information when you play our game.

## 2. Information We Collect

Our game may collect the following information:

- **Meta User ID:** Used to identify players and create or access their PlayFab accounts.
- **PlayFab ID:** Used to identify a player's game account.
- **Display Name:** Used to identify players in multiplayer sessions.
- **Game Data:** Information such as in-game currency and player progress.
- **Meta Username:** Used to display your name to others and allow others to report players when needed.

If Horizon usernames or profile photos are enabled in the future, this policy will be updated to explain their use.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage player accounts.
- Allow players to access their game progress.
- Support multiplayer functionality.
- Display player names in-game.
- Maintain and improve the game.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our game uses third-party services, including:

- **Meta Platforms:** For Meta account identification and platform services.
- **PlayFab:** For player accounts and game data.
- **Photon:** For multiplayer networking.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Player information is processed through the services listed above. We take reasonable steps to protect player information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

## 6. Data Deletion

Players can contact us to request deletion of their associated game data.

Requests can be made using the contact details below.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game develops. Any changes will be published on this page.
